MHN Media Archiver

An internal tool of MHN Window & Door Center, Yangon, Myanmar.

MHN Media Archiver saves the product photos and videos that MHN staff post in the company's own Telegram group into the company's own Google Drive folder, and keeps a list of them in the company's own Google Sheet. The files are used to make MHN's social-media videos.

It is used only by MHN Window & Door Center with its own Google account. It is not offered to the public.
