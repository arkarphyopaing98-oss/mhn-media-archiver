Privacy Policy

MHN Media Archiver · last updated 4 November 2026

Who we are

MHN Media Archiver is an internal tool of MHN Window & Door Center, Yangon, Myanmar. Only MHN uses it, with MHN's own Google account.

What Google data the app uses

  • Google Drive: uploads company photos and videos into MHN's own folder, reads that folder's files, and makes small preview/split copies of large videos in the same folder.
  • Google Sheets: writes one row per file (date, sender name, file link, product category, short description) into MHN's own spreadsheet.

The app does not read any other Drive files, email, contacts or calendar.

How the data is used and shared

  • The data is used only to organise MHN's product footage and make MHN's own marketing videos.
  • Pictures may be sent to an AI service (Google Gemini) only to describe and sort them.
  • We do not sell, rent or share the data with anyone else, and we do not use it for advertising.
  • Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and deletion

Files stay in MHN's own Google Drive and Sheet. Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
